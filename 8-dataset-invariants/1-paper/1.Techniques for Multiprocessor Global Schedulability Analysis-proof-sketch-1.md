====section==== First Miss Geometry (canonical setup)
- (comment) Fix target task task_k whose job is the first deadline miss.
- (comment) t_deadline : absolute deadline of the missed job.
- (comment) t_arrival  : release time of that job.
- (comment) t0         : latest instant < t_arrival with at most (num_cpus - 1) ready jobs.

- (comment) miss_work_condition (Eq. (2) form): total required work in the constructed window > num_cpus * (ak_interval + block_len_k).

====section==== Aggregated Interference Bound (Eq. (7) form)
- (comment) order_stats_top_deltas : for nonnegative deltas, the sum of any (num_cpus - 1) ≤ sum of top (num_cpus - 1).
